QUICK DEPLOYMENT GUIDE - GitHub Pages
======================================

What you'll get: https://yourusername.github.io/sports-signup/

STEPS:

1. CREATE REPOSITORY
   - Go to github.com, sign in
   - Click + (top right) → New repository
   - Name: sports-signup
   - Public: Yes
   - Click "Create repository"

2. UPLOAD FILES
   - Click "uploading an existing file"
   - Drag these 3 files into the box:
     • index.html
     • football_signup.html
     • netball_signup.html
   - Click "Commit changes"

3. ENABLE GITHUB PAGES
   - Go to Settings → Pages
   - Source: main branch
   - Click Save
   - Wait 2 minutes, refresh
   - Copy your live URL

4. SHARE
   Your URLs will be:
   - Main: https://yourusername.github.io/sports-signup/
   - Football: https://yourusername.github.io/sports-signup/football_signup.html
   - Netball: https://yourusername.github.io/sports-signup/netball_signup.html

DONE! Share the link with your team.

Note: Each person gets their own separate signup sheet.
Data does NOT sync between users.
Use CSV export to share rosters.
